Who are we

PT. Kreasi Kama Nusantara (Kamadig) is a growing Start-up focused on strategy & handling digital creative content utilizing advanced technology and research to broaden the market reach.

Kamadig established digital creative content as their tool for marketing campaigns and is trusted by both private and government institutions to provide comprehensive and scalable digital campaign management solutions. Now, we are coming up with the idea #GoFaster as the main concept of our partner’s happiness and satisfaction.

Our business strategy combines program development techniques, excel at applications and digital content with creative and innovative acquire strong qualitative and quantitative research and development

Our Methodology
Research-based output is our strength. Kamadig provides an integrated solution for the development and management of digital campaigns

Kamadig conducts a series of activities to explore the client’s condition through what has been done, conduct related research to produce strategies, and provide recommendations for digital creative campaign solutions.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a leading content creation company to support the digital economy in Indonesia

Mission

  1. Contributes significantly to the dissemination of trusted and reliable information on products, services, and public information
  2. Provides a good collaboration, new ideas concept as well as innovation of digital marketing to our clients and partners
  3. Provides chance and work opportunities for fresh graduates
